A Little About Our Story

South Park is an outrageous animated comedy created by Trey Parker and Matt Stone. The show premiered on Comedy Central on August 13, 1997, introducing audiences to a small, unusual town in the mountains of Colorado.

The series follows four young friends: Eric Cartman, Stanley Marsh, Kenneth McCormick, and Kyle Broflovski. Together, they find themselves caught up in ridiculous adventures, unexpected trouble, and some of the strangest situations imaginable.

Known for its foul-mouthed characters, dark humor, outrageous jokes, and hilarious social commentary, South Park isn't your typical cartoon.

So get ready to meet the gang, explore their world, and discover why South Park has entertained audiences for decades!
